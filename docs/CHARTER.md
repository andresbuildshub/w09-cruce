# Charter — w09-cruce · "Cruce"

> The constitution of this product. When my agent and I disagree about a feature at 2am, this is the referee. Written BEFORE the first line of code.

**What it exists to do:** show a call-center worker in Mexico, honestly, **where people who did their job went next**. Each crossing comes with what the data can and can't say, and with the verified door they'd have to pass to get there.

**For whom:** the 40-year-old who is still at a call center today (izzi, a bank, a telco, an outsourcer), before the layoff, not after it. Secondary: the person who just got laid off.

**What it will never do:**
1. **Hide the fall.** If the most common observed exit pays less or leads to informality, the screen says so. A map that hides the fall is Synchia lying to Elsa.
2. **Tell anyone where they *should* go.** "A dónde fueron personas como tú," never "a dónde deberías ir." The choice belongs to the person.
3. **Score a human.** No ranking by anyone's metrics, no pass/fail, no profile that an employer could read.
4. **Let a possible crossing borrow the credibility of an observed one.** Two layers, two labels: *observado* (INEGI's ENOE panel) and *camino posible, no observado*.
5. **Show a cell under 10 people.** Below 10 the screen says "No sabemos: muy pocos casos," with no wage and no arrow.
6. **Show a door without a source and a date.** Every requirement (license, certification) has a URL and "verificado el." **The LLM never writes a door.**
7. **List vacancies.** A door is a requirement, not an opening. The day it lists jobs, it's a job board (forbidden).

**Long view (3 sentences):** In three years, Cruce is the public crossing map for every occupation that AI exposure indices flag. It runs on pooled ENOE panels plus IMSS records, with the coding noise measured and published instead of hidden. Every door links to the state's own live-observed proofs (CONOCER standards like EC0784), so a displaced worker can carry a human-witnessed proof of what they already know into the crossing. The walls stay where they are: no hidden fall, no human scored, no door without a source.
